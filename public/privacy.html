Privacy Policy

My Book Journal · Last updated October 7, 2026

My Book Journal is a small personal web app for rating and reviewing books. It has no server of its own: it runs entirely in your browser and stores your journal in your own Google account.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for:

  • Your basic profile (name, email address, profile picture), shown in the app so you know which account you are using.
  • Google Drive access limited to files the app creates (the drive.file scope). The app uses it to create one spreadsheet, “My Book Journal”, and to read and update it. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

Where your data is stored

  • Your books, ratings, reviews and tags are stored in the “My Book Journal” spreadsheet in your Google Drive. You own it and can view, edit, share, download or delete it at any time.
  • Your browser keeps a local copy of your journal and your sign-in token so the app opens quickly and works offline. Signing out removes the token.
  • The app's developer does not operate a server and does not receive, collect, store or sell your data.

Third-party services

  • Google (sign-in, Google Sheets and Google Drive APIs), under Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Open Library (openlibrary.org and covers.openlibrary.org): your book search text is sent to Open Library to find books and covers. See the Internet Archive's terms and privacy policy.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data such as IP addresses, under GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Google API Services User Data Policy

My Book Journal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the app's features to you and is never transferred to anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Removing access and deleting data

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete your journal by deleting the “My Book Journal” spreadsheet from your Google Drive.
